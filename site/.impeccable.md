# Design Context — AFFIX

- **Audience:** Staff engineers, researchers, and technical founders evaluating production agent frameworks
- **Use cases:** Compare AFFIX and RHS scores, read methodology, inspect failure-mode coverage, choose a stack by persona
- **Brand personality:** Institutional research ledger — cool paper, deep ink, sparse carmine accent. Specimens and tables, not startup theatre.
- **Tone:** Scientific, restrained, postable. No neon, no orange glow, no section-number marketing chrome.
