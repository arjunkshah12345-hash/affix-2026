# Design Context — AFFIX

- **Audience:** Founders and staff engineers choosing an AI agent framework
- **Use cases:** Scan rankings, understand rewrite risk, pick a stack by persona
- **Brand personality:** Dark YC startup — near-black canvas, warm orange, dither-kit as craft signature. Sparse, decisive, high-agency.
- **Tone:** Confident research drop. Scientific claims, startup-grade visual presence. Dither texture on CTAs, hero field, and charts—not decoration for its own sake.
