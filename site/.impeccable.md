# Design Context — AFFIX

- **Audience:** Founders and staff engineers choosing an AI agent framework
- **Use cases:** Scan rankings, understand rewrite risk, pick a stack by persona
- **Brand personality:** YC-startup confidence — sparse black canvas, warm orange accent, decisive typography, dither texture as product craft
- **Tone:** Modern, editorial, high-agency. Not neon cyberpunk. Not purple AI slop.
