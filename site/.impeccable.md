# Design Context — AFFIX

- **Audience:** Founders and staff engineers choosing an AI agent framework
- **Use cases:** Scan the index, open dossiers, compare rewrite hazard
- **Brand personality:** Dark YC index desk — fixed ink rail, full-bleed dither specimen stage, orange as signal not wallpaper
- **Tone:** Spare, decisive, research-grade. Composition ≠ prior “bottom glow + Rank frameworks by what lasts” layout.
- **Differentiation:** The hero *is* the leading score specimen on a dither field; navigation lives in a left rail, not a sticky top bar.
